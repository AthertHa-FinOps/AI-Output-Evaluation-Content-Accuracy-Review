# Evidence, ownership and limitations

## Evidence classification

| Classification | In this repository | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid work** | Regulated real estate agreements, documents, records and follow-up described from career history | Not represented as paid AI annotation, Python engineering or enterprise CRM administration |
| **Executed sample** | Python reconciliation and automated unit tests on fictional files, plus CI | Only tests the submitted synthetic data and code; not production accuracy |
| **AI-generated reference material** | 30 evaluation cases, 10 paired comparisons, dimensional rubric and drafted rationales | Not an independent personal evaluation, certified benchmark or claimed hiring assessment |
| **Author narrative** | Earlier case-study notes about technical document reviews | Historical source claims have not all been independently verified against their original versions |
| **Not established** | Paid AI evaluator experience, volume-based QA performance, model-to-model quality rankings, human inter-rater agreement | No metrics or claims supplied |

## How to use responsibly

- On a résumé, present the case bank as a **structured synthetic evaluation exercise**, not thirty completed paid reviews.
- If you independently review examples, record dated decisions in the worksheet without overwriting the model reference key.
- The rubric is a reasoned model, not an industry certification or validated benchmark.
- For privacy-sensitive examples, all names, records and numbers are deliberately fictional.
- The repository does not prove an employer would hire the candidate. It can give a recruiter interview questions with inspectable evidence.

No provenance metadata is removed or falsified. No proprietary AI detector, outside reviewer, production client system or real-world accuracy certification was used.
