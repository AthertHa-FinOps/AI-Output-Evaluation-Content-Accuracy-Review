This portfolio has two kinds of material that should not be confused.

**Paid work.** My real estate roles involve reviewing agreements, amendments, disclosures, identity documents and financial records, identifying discrepancies and coordinating corrections. At HOOPP (June 2005–June 2008), I worked as a Project Associate / Analyst (Contract) in QRM, checking pension accounts and calculation results during migration-related defect-remediation projects.
