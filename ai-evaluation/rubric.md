# Review rubric

| Decision | Requirement |
|---|---|
| PASS | Output matches the supplied source and instruction. |
| FAIL / HIGH | Materially incorrect amount or failure to mask an identifier. |
| FAIL / MEDIUM | Unsupported claim that a design or process was deployed. |
| FAIL / LOW | Minor formatting failure where an exact format was required. |

Quote the fictional source. State the observed contradiction and recommended correction. A planned action must not be reported as executed.
