## Review procedure

1. Open [answer-key-free individual cases](unscored_cases.jsonl) and [answer-key-free A/B pairs](unscored_pairs.jsonl). Do **not** open the matching reference labels during that pass.
2. Decide whether each **candidate response** is ACCEPT or REJECT; for pairs, choose A, B, TIE or NEITHER. Explain the decisive source sentence, number, privacy rule or instruction.
3. Record separately whether the underlying fact is verifiable or **requires more evidence**. Do not reject a cautious answer simply because the business fact cannot be established.
4. Record the real date and any uncertainty. For Spanish cases, assess the original meaning and English rendering directly.
5. Compare with the AI-assisted reference afterward. Retain your original judgment and note any disagreement. Do not backfill a result you did not personally reach.

These files hide the reference labels, but they cannot make a test genuinely blind if you have already read or remember the answers. In that situation, describe the activity as a **reference-hidden self-check**, not independent blind performance.
