# Additional practice: difficult response-review decisions

These **8 individual tasks and 4 A/B comparisons** were prepared with AI assistance for practice. They are fictional, **not actual LLM outputs**, and have no supplied answer key. No personal verdicts have been entered.

[Single-response tasks](cases.jsonl) · [A/B comparisons](pairs.jsonl) · [Personal review worksheet](../OWNER_REVIEW_WORKSHEET.md) · [Rubric](../rubric.md)

## How to use these

1. Read the source and exact task first, without requesting an AI-generated rating.
2. Rate the single response or choose A, B, TIE or NEITHER. Cite the decisive words or figures.
3. **Write at least two original rationales in Spanish** for Spanish-source cases or pairs. Include the precise regional or register issue; do not just say the translation sounds wrong.
4. Explain one close comparison in ordinary English. A preference may be weak or a tie; only report a numeric strength if the task defines a scale.
5. Record the true review date and what you initially decided. These tasks were developed with AI assistance, so do not describe them as unseen real model outputs or calibrated professional evaluations.

For stronger evidence, use the separate [actual-model-output procedure](../REAL_OUTPUT_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) on permitted material and save the first rating before asking an AI system to review it. These practice tasks are not a substitute for that work.
