# Additional review practice — without reference ratings

These **8 individual tasks and 4 A/B comparisons** were prepared with AI assistance. They are fictional examples, not captured LLM outputs. No preferred answer, dimension scores or completed personal reviews are included.

## Your review procedure

1. Read the source, instruction and response before requesting any AI assessment. The task descriptions identify the subject matter but do **not** provide a reference judgment.
2. For a single response choose ACCEPT or REJECT and rate each applicable dimension. For pairs choose A, B, TIE or NEITHER, explaining the source detail that matters.
3. Write at least **two original rationales in Spanish** for Spanish-source or Spanish-output tasks. Include register, regional meaning, or conditional wording where relevant.
4. Record the date and original decision in the [blank log](../owner_review_log_template.csv). Do not fill any rating, date or rationale on someone else's behalf.
5. Explain any uncertain or close decision in plain language. No numerical preference-strength metric is implied by these tasks.

These constructed exercises can help you practise the method, but they cannot establish performance on genuinely unseen model responses. Use the [model-output capture protocol](../REAL_OUTPUT_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) to collect such evidence when permitted.
